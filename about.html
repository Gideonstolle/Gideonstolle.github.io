About us

Creative work with a human rhythm

Atelier Stolle is a place for art, craft, conversation, and the kind of careful making that rewards attention.

Atelier Stolle was founded on February 28, 1993.

Our story

We bring artists, visitors, and neighbors together through workshops, exhibitions, and small events. The atelier is built around curiosity, skill-sharing, and generous creative exchange.

Atelier Stolle organizes exhibitions of artworks by others, creates jewelry and art objects, and carries out goldsmithing work. We also work as a handyman business on projects where material, form, and craftsmanship come together. In everything we do, we focus on craft, sustainable materials, and creating personal, unique results.

Visit us

Stop by for an exhibition, join a workshop, or follow along as the shop grows into a place for limited objects and studio editions.
